Under the hood

How it works

A diffusion model turns your text into video frames through an iterative denoising process.

01

Text Encoding

Your prompt is tokenised and converted to vector embeddings by a CLIP text encoder.

02

Diffusion

Starting from random noise, the DPM-Solver scheduler iteratively denoises the video frames using your prompt as a guide.

03

VAE Decode

A Variational Autoencoder converts the latent frames into pixel-space images.

04

MP4 Export

Individual frames are assembled and saved as an MP4 at 8 fps using imageio-ffmpeg.
